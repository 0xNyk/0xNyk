- **Market Making & Trading Infra** — DLMM/CLOB systems, Jupiter integration, Meteora liquidity automation, HFT pipelines on Solana
- **[Pump.science](https://builderz.dev/work/pump-science)** — Gamified longevity research for DeSci on Solana (built for Molecule DAO, $1M+ volume, featured at Breakpoint 2024)
